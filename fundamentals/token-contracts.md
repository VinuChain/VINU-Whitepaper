# Token Contracts

## VINU is Multichain

$VINU is native to the VITE Chain, and bridged to other chains such as VC, BSC, ETH, MATIC, BASE, OKTC, & WAN.

The top wallets on every other chain except VITE Chain are the [Bridge Cold & Hot wallets](https://www.leewayhertz.com/hot-and-cold-wallet-architecture/). Balances in them are not in circulation. They just help investors transfer $VINU 1:1 between VITE and Other Chains. No new supply is ever created.

Read more about our bridge here: [How to use the VINU Bridge](https://medium.com/@VitaInu/how-to-bridge-vinu-to-binance-smart-chain-trade-on-pancakeswap-bc2e08fbdb9b)

Read more about our supply here: [VINU Supply Explained](https://medium.com/@VitaInu/vinu-supply-figures-explained-a97ef618df5e)

Refer to [CMC](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/vita-inu/) or [CG](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/vita-inu) for our verified Market Cap & Supply.
